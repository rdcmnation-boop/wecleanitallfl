# Project EARTH ONE — Vision
_We Clean It All Global Initiative · Version 1 · 2026-11-02 · Concept and design only. No production system changed, no infrastructure deployed or purchased._

## The concept
EARTH ONE connects people, AI, autonomous robotics, smart buildings, cleaning and maintenance services, and environmental intelligence to help communities become cleaner and more sustainable.

| Layer | Role |
|---|---|
| **We Clean It All** | the commercial platform and the business that pays for everything |
| **EARTH ONE** | the global environmental technology initiative built on that platform |
| **AI** | the intelligence layer: it analyzes, predicts, drafts and flags |
| **Robotics** | the physical operations layer: real machines from real manufacturers, under human supervision |
| **Humans** | essential partners: they do the work, supervise, decide and stay accountable |

_One planet. One connected mission. Millions of opportunities._

## The foundation comes first
1. Build the **profitable Miami-Dade + Broward marketplace** (MASTER_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md).
2. **Prove the technology** through the Innovation Lab waves (quality, building profiles, intelligence, impact, academy, growth, robotics pilot).
3. **Grow EARTH ONE** from that real foundation, one measured pilot at a time.

EARTH ONE never puts the cleaning business at risk. It uses the same database, roles, ledger and agents. Its stages run behind feature flags, and none starts until the stage before it is proven.

## Principles
1. **Reuse the approved architecture.** No second platform.
2. **Preserve the cleaning business.** Its journey, prices and data are not changed by EARTH ONE work.
3. **Many countries, many rules.** Every country/state/city has its own legal, tax, data, labor and robotics settings. Nothing launches in a jurisdiction without its review.
4. **Authorized data only.** Every sensor, meter, camera or city data feed needs a signed data agreement with a purpose and an end date.
5. **No invented measurements.** Every environmental number carries `MEASURED` or `ESTIMATED`, its method and its source. A saving is only claimed when a measured baseline and a measured result exist.
6. **Real and simulated robotics are kept apart:** separate flags, separate data, separate metrics, and simulation never runs in a real market.
7. **People approve sensitive actions:** contracts, bids, prices, spending, data sharing, public claims, robot deployment, penalties and refunds.
8. **Protect people and property:** least data, encrypted secrets, private media, no face recognition, retention limits.

## Human and AI responsibilities
| Area | AI does | Humans do |
|---|---|---|
| Operations | plans routes and crews, predicts delays, raises alerts | approve plans, dispatch help, handle incidents |
| Robotics | suggests robot-eligible tasks, monitors mission progress | supervise every mission, stop/escalate, approve deployments |
| Environment | analyzes data, drafts reports, suggests improvements | approve every public claim and report |
| Maintenance | predicts likely issues with a confidence level | inspect, decide, repair (licensed trades) |
| Partnerships | researches, drafts proposals and bids | negotiate, approve, sign |
| Money | calculates estimates | approve prices, refunds, spending, payouts rules |
| Data | applies the agreement's rules automatically | sign data agreements, approve any sharing |

## Success looks like
Measured, not claimed: paid recurring contracts beyond home cleaning; measured reductions at real client sites; robot missions completed safely under supervision; partners transacting on the marketplace; each new jurisdiction passing its launch checklist.
