# We Clean It All — Innovation and Future Technology Lab
_Version 1 · 2026-11-02. Design only._

**Guiding principle (new):** We Clean It All becomes an **operating system for the cleaning industry**, not just another cleaning app: one connected ecosystem, several revenue streams, and technology that helps people do better work.
**Business rule:** protect the business while building the future. Each innovation below starts only after the core journey (MASTER_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY.md) works profitably in Miami-Dade and Broward, unless it directly supports that journey.

## How the seven innovations plug in
None needs a new system. Each one extends a module that already exists or is planned.

| 4 | **Quality Guarantee Engine** | Provider tools + trust & safety | checklists, before/after photos, inspections, missed-task reports, corrective work orders | fewer refunds; "guaranteed" plan | Phase 3 (part of the core journey) | Checklist + photos + 48-hour missed-task button |

## Safety and honesty rules for the Lab
1. **Privacy:** the Intelligence Network uses only aggregates (no addresses, names or photos), with a minimum group size before anything is shown. Nothing identifiable is shared without the customer's written permission.
2. **Quality:** photos and AI only **flag** jobs for human review. Nobody is penalized or refunded automatically.
3. **Growth Engine:** AI researches public business information and drafts proposals. **You approve** every send, price and contract. Contact only prospects who opted in or are legitimately allowed under email/SMS rules (CAN-SPAM, TCPA).
4. **Robots:** mission requests only. Manufacturer safety systems are never overridden. A human supervisor is assigned to every robot job. The simulator is always labeled.
5. **Impact:** every number says whether it's measured or estimated. Nothing is called "verified" without evidence.
6. **Cost:** each feature lists its AI/storage cost before it's switched on. AI stays under the monthly cap.

## Recommended order
**4 (Quality) → 1 (Digital twin) → 6 (Academy) → 5 (Growth) → 2 (Intelligence) → 7 (Impact) → 3 (Mission Control)**
Quality and the digital twin directly improve the core journey and margins. The Academy improves provider quality. Growth brings commercial revenue. Intelligence and Impact need real data first. Mission Control needs a real robot partner.
